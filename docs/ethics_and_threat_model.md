# CompGraphRAG Ethical Scoping, Threat Model & Regulatory Governance

## 1. Ethical Scoping & Human-in-the-Loop Governance

CompGraphRAG is explicitly designed as a **Decision Support System (DSS)** for compliance officers, legal auditors, and enterprise risk managers.

> [!CAUTION]
> **EXCLUDED SCOPE / SOLE-LIABILITY PROHIBITION:**
> CompGraphRAG MUST NOT be deployed as an autonomous, sole-basis decision-maker for issuing legal penalties, reporting regulatory breaches, or denying medical care access. All determinations marked as `REQUIRES-REVIEW` or generated by the system MUST undergo mandatory human review by a qualified compliance professional prior to taking legal action.

### Ethical Safeguards:
1. **Mandatory Human-in-the-Loop (HITL):** Low-confidence determinations (where conformal set $|C(q)| > 1$ or ECE $> 0.05$) are routed to human auditor review.
2. **Deterministic Precedence:** Symbolic compliance rules override statistical LLM generation when statutory exceptions or mandatory prohibitions are triggered.
3. **Auditable $\pi$-Path Walk:** System outputs must include step-by-step natural language path walks ($\pi$-paths) grounded in statutory TBox entities.

## 2. Threat Model & Data Security Boundaries

### Security Baseline (HIPAA 45 CFR §164.312 Compliance)

1. **Zero Unencrypted PHI Transmission:** All vector embeddings and knowledge graph nodes containing Protected Health Information (PHI) are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
2. **On-Premise / Sovereign Enclave Deployment:** CompGraphRAG supports isolated air-gapped deployment to prevent data leakage to third-party public LLM endpoints.
3. **Audit Trails & Log Integrity:** All retrieval queries, rule triggers, and conformal uncertainty determinations are immutably logged for regulatory audit inspection.
