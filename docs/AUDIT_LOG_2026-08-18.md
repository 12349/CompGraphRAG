## 2. Reproducibility Verdict

Three consecutive runs: **bit-for-bit identical**. The harness is fully deterministic under the installed environment (hash pseudo-embeddings, MD5 seeds, fixed path ordering).

**IMPORTANT CAVEAT**: The encoder in use is a hash pseudo-embedding fallback, not `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`. Installing `sentence-transformers` would change cosine similarity scores and could change accuracy outcomes. The numbers above are real executed output, not neural-embedding output. This is flagged as an open limitation.
